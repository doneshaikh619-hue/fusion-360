About Fusion 360

Located at Showroom No. 7 in the prestigious GW Tower near Bilawal Roundabout in Clifton Block 2, Fusion 360 brings together upscale culinary comfort with fine dine elegance. Known for its double-smashed beef burgers, flame-grilled chicken, rich pasta specialties, and grand BBQ platters, Fusion 360 serves discerning food lovers across Karachi with both a welcoming dine-in ambiance and reliable late-night delivery.

Whether you are stopping by for a casual late-night dinner with friends, craving our signature C3 burgers, or ordering a loaded sharing platter for family, our kitchen maintains rigorous quality standards from our signature marination to table presentation.
